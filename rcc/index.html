Why parents choose us

  • A written test every Saturday, and the marks reach you the same evening.

    Twenty marks, forty minutes, on that week's chapters. We send the marks on WhatsApp by 8 pm, so Sunday can be spent on what went wrong.

    sample

  • Every notebook is checked in red pen, every week.

    Not a signature at the bottom of the page. Each wrong step is marked where it went wrong, and your child redoes it in class before going home.

    sample

  • Fifteen minutes with you on the first Sunday of every month.

    One family at a time, with the test register open on the table. You leave knowing which chapters are strong and which need work.

    sample

    Your child sits in one full class, in the batch they would join.

  3. Documents sample

Questions parents ask

Is the demo class really free?
Yes. One full class in the batch your child would join. There is no fee for it and nothing to sign before it. sample
Which boards do you teach?
CBSE, from the NCERT books your child uses at school. Tell us the school and we will follow its exam dates. sample
How many children are in one batch?
Twelve at the most. If a batch fills up we open a second one at another time rather than add chairs. sample
Do you teach in Hindi or in English?
Both. We explain in the language your child understands, and write in the language of the school's books. sample
How will I know how my child is doing?
Saturday's test marks reach you on WhatsApp the same evening, and we meet every parent on the first Sunday of the month. sample
What if my child misses a class?
Send a message on WhatsApp. The missed topic is covered again in Saturday's doubt hour, at no extra fee. sample
