Exterior detail

Foam, a hand wash, decontamination. Dried with air and soft towels, wheels and arches included.

Scroll to see it doneDrag to turn

Book this Ask about this

1Overview

A proper wash is slow on purpose. Grit is lifted off with foam before a mitt goes near the paint, each panel is washed by hand, and what washing cannot shift is taken out with clay and iron remover. The car is dried with warm air and soft towels, so nothing is dragged across it.

2What is included

  1. 1Snow foam pre-washLeft to dwell, then rinsed, so loose grit leaves before anything touches the paint.
  2. 2Two-bucket hand washA clean mitt, panel by panel, from the roof down.
  3. 3Wheels, tyres and archesCleaned on their own, with their own brushes.
  4. 4DecontaminationClay and iron remover for tar, fallout and bonded grit.
  5. 5Air and towel dryWarm air for the gaps, soft towels for the panels.
  6. 6Glass and trimGlass cleaned into the door shuts, trim dressed.

3How it goes

  1. 1

    Look it over.

    We walk round the car with you and note what it needs.

  2. 2

    Foam and rinse.

    Foam dwells on the paint and takes the loose dirt with it.

  3. 3

    Wash by hand.

    Two buckets, a clean mitt, one panel at a time.

  4. 4

    Decontaminate.

    Clay and iron remover lift what is bonded to the paint.

  5. 5

    Dry and check.

    Dried, then checked under the lights before it leaves.

The same car, the same light, photographed in the bay. Drag the tape.
