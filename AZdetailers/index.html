06Questions

Asked before booking.

What people ask before they book.

GeneralDo I need an appointment?

Appointments are strongly recommended, so the bay and the time your car needs are kept for it.

GeneralDo you work on luxury, exotic and specialty vehicles?

Yes. Daily drivers, luxury sedans, exotics and family SUVs all get the same care.

PPF spray & filmWill PeelClear damage my original paint?

No. It releases cleanly without adhesive, chemicals or heat, and the factory finish underneath stays exactly as it was.

PPF spray & filmCan I go through a car wash?

Touchless or hand washing is best. Brush car washes wear any paint finish, PeelClear included, and are best avoided.

Ceramic coatingHow long does Opti-Coat Pro last?

Opti-Coat comes with warrantied protection times: 2, 5, 7 or 10 years, or lifetime. You choose.

Ceramic coatingIs paint correction included?

Every ceramic coating package starts with a professional paint assessment and correction, so the coating bonds to the best surface the paint can give.

Paint correctionWill correction remove every scratch?

Anything in the clear coat, usually. A scratch that catches a fingernail may be through it, and polishing it out would take too much paint. We tell you which is which before we start.

Paint correctionIs correction safe for my paint?

Done right, yes. We measure the paint first and remove only what the defect needs. Factory clear coat has room for a few careful corrections in a car's life.

Window tintHow dark can I go?

That depends on the state and the window. We keep the front side windows to the legal limit and can go darker behind the driver. Ask us what applies to your car.

Window tintWhy are there bubbles after install?

A little haze or a few water pockets in the first days is moisture drying out through the film. It clears on its own. Keep the windows up for three to five days.

DetailingHow long does a complete detail take?

Most vehicles take between 3 and 6 hours, depending on size and condition.

DetailingDo your prices vary by vehicle size?

Yes. Pricing depends on the vehicle's size, its condition and the level of service. You get an accurate quote before any work begins.
