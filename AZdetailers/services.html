01Paint protection · Zone F, Leading edgesSpeciality

Protection that peels off clean.

PeelClear is sprayed on in a booth and cures to a true paint finish: no seams, no film edges, and it peels away without a trace.

PeelClear is a waterborne, peelable coating, sprayed on with the same equipment as automotive paint. It builds a thick, sandable layer over the factory finish that takes the rock chips, bugs and road rash instead of the clear coat, and resists UV and daily wear. It goes on clear, lightly tinted or in any colour, gloss, satin or matte, and when you want a change, or the car is sold, it peels off in large sections with no adhesive and no harm to the paint underneath. AZ Detailers still lays traditional clear film where it suits the car better.

What's included

  • Sprayed in a booth: every curve, no seams, no film edges
  • Clear, lightly tinted, or any colour in gloss, satin or matte
  • Sandable and polishable, like real paint
  • Ceramic coating can go on top
  • Peels off clean: 20-year peelability warranty from the maker

Questions about paint protection

Will PeelClear damage my original paint?

No. It releases cleanly without adhesive, chemicals or heat, and the factory finish underneath stays exactly as it was.

Can I go through a car wash?

Touchless or hand washing is best. Brush car washes wear any paint finish, PeelClear included, and are best avoided.

Can PeelClear be ceramic coated?

Yes. It is compatible with a ceramic coating on top.

02Ceramic coating · Zone P, Paint

Water beads, and rolls off.

Opti-Coat Pro: a silicon carbide coating that bonds to the clear coat, instead of sitting on top of it like wax.

It cannot simply wash away.

03Paint correction · Zone P, Paint

Paint, corrected.

Swirls, light scratches, oxidation and water spot etching, polished out of the clear coat for good. Not hidden under a wax.

Correction levels the clear coat with machine polishers and professional compounds, so defects are removed at their source instead of filled for a week. The paint is inspected under specialised light and its thickness measured first, so each panel gets the safest method that will work. It restores depth, clarity and gloss, and it is the step every coating at AZ Detailers starts from.

What's included

  • Inspection under specialised lighting
  • Wash, chemical decontamination and clay
  • Paint thickness measured
  • Machine polishing, in stages
  • Ready for ceramic or PeelClear

Defects it removes

  • Light surface scratches
  • Oxidation
  • Water spot etching
  • Holograms and buffer trails
  • Light scuffs and paint transfer

Questions about paint correction

Will correction remove every scratch?

Anything in the clear coat, usually. A scratch that catches a fingernail may be through it, and polishing it out would take too much paint. We tell you which is which before we start.

Is correction safe for my paint?

Done right, yes. We measure the paint first and remove only what the defect needs. Factory clear coat has room for a few careful corrections in a car's life.

How long does the result last?

The defects are gone for good. What brings swirls back is washing: a coating and a good wash routine keep the paint the way it leaves us.

04Window tint · Zone G, Glass

Cooler cabin. Clean edges.

Film on the inside of the glass, cut to each window, that blocks the UV and cuts the heat and the glare.

Tint is a film laid on the inside of the glass, cut to each window and shrunk to its curve. Carbon film darkens the glass and rejects a good share of the heat; ceramic film rejects far more of it, even in lighter shades, without interfering with phone or GPS signals. The shade is measured as visible light transmission: the lower the number, the darker the glass.

What's included

  • Glass cleaned and prepped
  • Film cut to each window, edges filed clean
  • Front windows kept to the legal limit
  • Curing advice and a check-back

Arizona allows film on the front side windows that lets more than 33% of light through; the rear side windows and back glass can go darker. The windshield takes a non-reflective strip above the AS-1 line.

Questions about window tint

How dark can I go?

That depends on the state and the window. We keep the front side windows to the legal limit and can go darker behind the driver. Ask us what applies to your car.

Why are there bubbles after install?

A little haze or a few water pockets in the first days is moisture drying out through the film. It clears on its own. Keep the windows up for three to five days.

Does ceramic film affect my phone or GPS?

No. Ceramic and carbon films contain no metal, so they do not block signals.

Can you tint the windshield?

A strip along the top is allowed in most states. A clear heat-rejecting film can go over the whole windshield where the law allows it.
